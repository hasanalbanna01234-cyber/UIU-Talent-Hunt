# UIU-Talent-Hunt
A web-based platform designed to discover, showcase, and celebrate the hidden talents of students at United International University (UIU).
The platform provides a space where talented individuals can share their creativity through video, audio, and text-based content, while allowing other users to interact with and appreciate their work.

##  Features

### 1. Video Talent Showcase

Users can showcase their talents through video content. The platform provides a dedicated space for users to share performances, presentations, creative work, and other video-based talents.

### 2. Audio Talent Showcase

Users can upload and share audio-based talents such as singing, instrumental performances, voice acting, podcasts, and other audio content.

### 3. Text Blogging

Users can express their creativity through written content. The platform supports text-based blogging where users can share stories, opinions, experiences, poems, articles, and other forms of creative writing.

### 4. Content Engagement

Users can interact with submitted content through likes, comments, and shares. These interactions help increase visibility and engagement for talented users.

### 5. Talent Leaderboard

The platform maintains a leaderboard that ranks users based on their overall engagement and performance. A developer-defined scoring system combines likes, comments, and shares to calculate leaderboard scores.

### 6. Engagement-Based Scoring

The platform uses a predefined scoring mechanism to evaluate user engagement. Likes, comments, and shares contribute different values toward a user's overall score.
